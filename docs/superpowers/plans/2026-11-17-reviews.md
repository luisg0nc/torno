# Reviews: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every mark of the person's can say **want** or **don't want**. "Review this build" opens round `R<n>` on the
build on show: marks made while it is open are its items, held back until Send review puts them in one prompt with one
review line. After a later build the round panel takes OK or Still wrong for each item. Still wrong items go straight
into the next round. Each round is a frame on the board, and Claude reads every round through `marks`.

**Architecture:**
- `intent`, `round` and `judged` live on marks, and a short `rounds` list lives in the same `marks.json`. `MarkStore`
  stays its one writer. Pure round rules (names, counts, what is held) are in a new `shared/reviews.ts`.
- `main/reviews/ipc.ts` handles `marks:round`, `marks:setIntent`, `marks:roundOut` and `marks:judge`. It finds the
  build on show in the history and stars it.
- `contextFor` holds a round's items back, as it does an open sketch. The round being sent (`sending`, main's, in
  memory) releases them with the review line, and `delivered` sets `sentAt`.
- The board's `reconcile` learns round frames (`kind: 'frame'`, `by: 'torno'`, `source: 'R1'`).
- In the window, a small `useReview` store holds the open bar and its chosen intent. The bar sits over the view beside
  `AlignBar` and `RegionBar`, the round panel is in `ui/review/`, and the chip is in `Composer.tsx` beside the board's.

**Tech stack:** Electron, TypeScript, React, three.js, vitest, Playwright (hidden window).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-reviews-design.md` (read it whole, "Builder's questions, settled" most of
all; the mockups are Review-A, Review-B and Review-C on the design canvas, row 6).

## Global Constraints

- Main is the only writer of `marks.json`: one change at a time, atomic, through `MarkStore.change`. Rounds live in that
  file. There is no second store.
- Round ids are `R<n>` per project, given by main from `nextRound`, never reused. That holds after a round is dropped
  and after a pull.
- `round` and `judged` join `MAIN_ONLY_KEYS`. Main sets `round` when the person makes a mark in the open round of that
  mark's layout, and when Still wrong carries an item. Main sets `judged` only from the round panel's OK and Still
  wrong. Neither is ever taken from the window or a tool.
- A round's drafts are **held** until Send review: left out of every prompt, never marked sent, and said as held. "Send
  as plain marks" (or removing the chip) releases them as ordinary drafts.
- Claude's marks stay plain. They never join a round, and `markIntent` of an agent mark is always `null`, a shape's
  `add`/`remove` included. `add_mark` and `add_sketch` take no review intent.
- No new colours. Marks keep their author's colour. Don't want is hatched with `−` in its chip, want is solid with `+`.
  `−` and `+` are set in the monospace face. Colours come from tokens and `viewport/theme.ts` only.
- One open round per project. A round never changes the model.
- New keys: `marks.review` and the bar's two intent keys are in scope `window`. Never `[` or `]` (the align patch and
  the region brush hold them). `keys.test.ts` checks every scope.
- UI text is in sentence case. Numbers and refs are in the monospace face, through `shared/format.ts`. No per-frame
  state in React.
- Tests never open a visible window, never call `page.screenshot()`, never use the clipboard, and never run the real
  `claude` (`TORNO_AGENT_CMD` is the stand-in).
- Commits end with the `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer, as this repo's `CLAUDE.md` asks.
- Before changing a file, read the `.claude/rules/` files whose `paths:` cover it: `marks-store`, `marks-window`,
  `board`, `builds-history`, `mcp-tools`, `mcp-read-tools`, `agent-prompt`, `keys`, `chat-pane`,
  `chat-cards-and-refs`. A change to what torno does changes the manual (`docs/manual.md`) and the rule that says it,
  in the same commit.

## Review Focus

1. **A prompt typed while a round is open:** the round's drafts are not sent and not marked sent, and the line under
   the prompt says `R1 is in review, not sent`. Other drafts go as usual. After Send review, the next prompt carries
   the review line and the items. `sentAt` is set only once that prompt is delivered (Task 3).
2. **OK all against the round's OK:** "OK all" and "To review" acknowledge a round item but never set `judged`, and the
   panel shows it as "OK (accepted)". Only the panel's OK and Still wrong set `judged` (Tasks 1 and 7).
3. **A pull brings a `marks.json` with rounds whose build is not local**, or whose local `h41` has another `sources`
   hash: the file reads, nothing is starred, the panel says "the reviewed build is not on this computer" and Compare is
   off. Two open rounds in a pulled file leave one open, and a mark naming a missing round is plain (Tasks 1 and 7).
4. **Deleting a round item, then undo:** the mark comes back with its `round`, `intent` and `judged`. The round's counts
   are as before, and its card goes back into the round's frame (Tasks 1 and 5).
5. **A frame of the person's holding a mark card:** when that mark joins a round or is carried into the next one, its
   card stays in the person's frame. Only a card placed for the first time, or one in the previous round's frame, goes
   into the round's frame (Task 5).

**Interfaces (produces):**
- In `shared/marks.ts`:
  - `type ReviewIntent = 'want' | 'dont'`
  - `interface Judged { verdict: 'ok' | 'still-wrong'; build: string /* h42 */ }`
  - `MarkBase` gains `round?: string` and `judged?: Judged`. Every kind but `shape` gains `intent?: ReviewIntent`.
    A shape keeps `intent: ShapeIntent`, so `MarkBase` cannot carry the field itself.
  - `markIntent(mark: Mark): ReviewIntent | null`:
    - `null` for every agent mark
    - for a shape, `add` → `'want'`, `remove` → `'dont'`, and anything else → `null`
    - for any other kind, its `intent ?? null`
  - `MAIN_ONLY_KEYS` gains `'round'` and `'judged'`.
  - `MarksFile` gains `rounds?: Round[]` and `nextRound?: number`.
- In `shared/reviews.ts`:
  - `ROUND_ID = /^R[1-9]\d{0,14}$/`
  - `interface Round { id: string; build: string; sources: string; line: string; layout: string; startedAt: string; endedAt?: string; sentAt?: string; plain?: string; from?: string }`.
    `plain` is when the round was let go as plain marks. The spec has no field for that, and `contextFor` needs it.
  - `interface RoundsView { rounds: Round[]; sending: string | null }`
  - `openRound(rounds): Round | undefined`: the one with no `endedAt`
  - `roundItems(marks, id): Mark[]`
  - `roundCounts(marks, id): { dont: number; want: number; plain: number }`
  - `reviewName(round, marks): string`, e.g. `Review of h41 · 3 − · 2 +`. A count of 0 is left out.
  - `isHeld(mark, view): boolean`: a draft whose round has no `sentAt`, no `plain`, and is not `view.sending`
- New `MarkStore` methods. Each is one `change`, and each refuses with a `Refusal` that names the mark or the round:
  - `rounds(objectId): Promise<RoundsView>`
  - `setIntent(objectId, id, intent: ReviewIntent | null): Promise<Mark>`:
    - It works on the person's marks only.
    - On a shape it writes `add` or `remove`. `null` on a shape is refused, saying keep-out and reference are set in
      its fields.
    - A sent mark becomes a draft. It already has `sentBefore`, so `news` says `changed`.
    - It never sets `round`.
  - `startRound(objectId, of: Pick<Round, 'layout' | 'build' | 'sources' | 'line'>): Promise<Round>`. When a round is
    already open, it returns that round unchanged.
  - `endRound(objectId, id)`. Ending a round with no items drops it, and its id is not given again.
  - `reopenRound(objectId, id)`: refused for a sent round, or while another round is open
  - `carryRound(objectId, id, of)`: opens the round whose `from` is `id`, making it on `of` if there is none
  - `sendRound(objectId, id)` and `plainRound(objectId, id)`. Both end the round. `sendRound` sets `sending`, which is
    in memory per object, forgotten with `forget`, and told with `changed`. `plainRound` sets `plain` and clears
    `sending`.
  - `roundSent(objectId, id)`: sets `sentAt` and clears `sending`
  - `roundOut(objectId, markId)`: clears the mark's `round`
  - `judge(objectId, markId, verdict, at: { build: string; note?: string; next: Pick<Round, 'layout' | 'build' | 'sources' | 'line'> })`:
    - It needs a mark resolved by the agent that is in a sent round.
    - `ok` sets `judged` and `acknowledged`.
    - `still-wrong` sets `judged`, makes the mark a draft with `reopened`, and appends `Still wrong in h42: <note>`
      to its note. It also moves the mark's `round` into the next round, which is made here when missing: on `next`,
      `from` the judged round, ended and unsent, so its items are held.
- `add`, `addNote` and `saveSketch` (new sketches only) set `round` when the author is the person and the open round's
  `layout` equals the mark's. `addRegion` goes through `add`.
- `MarkEvents['changed']` gains `rounds: Round[]` and `sending: string | null`.
- The sanitiser drops a bad `intent`, `round` or `judged` from a mark and keeps the mark. It drops a malformed round,
  ends every open round but the newest, clears a `round` that names no round, and raises `nextRound` above every `R<n>`.
